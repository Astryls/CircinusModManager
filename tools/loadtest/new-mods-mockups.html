New mods — three ways

Same data in each: 1 204 mods installed, 3 of them arrived since you last looked. Left is the main list (By phase), right is what the same idea looks like in a split pane and in What changes.

A

Marked where they already are

Nothing moves. A new mod keeps its place in the load order and gets a green edge, a faint tint and a NEW tag. The toolbar counts them and steps between them. Reading the list is unchanged — you just see which rows are new — but with three new mods among twelve hundred you are relying on the counter to find them.

B

A section of their own, at the top

New mods are lifted into a New section above the phases, so you see them without hunting. They also stay faintly in their real place, so you can still tell where each one will load. Got it dismisses the section and the marks. Costs one heading of vertical space and shows each new mod twice.

C

A tab of their own, and one dot

The list itself is untouched: a new mod gets a single green dot in the Changed slot and nothing else. Finding them is what the New tab is for, beside All / Active / Inactive — one click and the list is the new mods, with every column and every action still working. Quietest in the list, one click away; but a new mod you scroll past is easy to miss.
